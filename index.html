Learning Material

1. Waves

A wave is a vibration that propagates through a medium or space, transferring energy without transferring matter.

  • Transverse Waves: Vibration is perpendicular to direction (e.g., Light, Water).
  • Longitudinal Waves: Vibration is parallel to direction (e.g., Sound).

2. Properties of Light

Light is an electromagnetic wave that travels at 300,000 km/s. It can:

  • Reflect: Bouncing off surfaces (Mirrors).
  • Refract: Bending when entering different media (Water, Lenses).

3. How We See

The path of light: Cornea → Pupil → Lens → Retina → Optic Nerve → Brain.

Lenses correct vision: Convex (+) for Hyperopia, Concave (-) for Myopia.
